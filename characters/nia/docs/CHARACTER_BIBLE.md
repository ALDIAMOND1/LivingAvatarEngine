# Nia — Character Bible / Creative Direction

## Positioning
Nia is a composed, warm, observant virtual human. She should read as a genuine webcam-presenter, not as a game avatar or an exaggerated animated cartoon. She is an original fictional character; the reference screenshot only conveys studio portrait realism.

## Original appearance
Adult woman with warm deep-brown skin, natural small-scale texture and pores, dark-brown eyes, subtle realistic highlights, neat side-parted braids drawn backward, delicate earrings, simple charcoal shirt. Soft gray neutral studio background. Soft key light and modest fill preserve skin details and believable shadows. Framing favors full face, neck and upper shoulders, with room for slight pose changes. Distinct facial proportions must be established by a **new original portrait** rather than trying to trace the reference.

## Baseline face
Relaxed eyelids, natural slight asymmetry, almost-neutral mouth with a hint of warmth. Avoid fixed oversized smile, wide staring eyes, and perfectly symmetrical involuntary movements.

## Natural movement grammar
- Always: minute breathing and posture changes, rare stabilization drift.
- Blink: irregular intervals, occasional quick double blink, no rhythmic looping.
- Gaze: brief saccades; pupils and eyeball move before the head follows; return to attentive eye contact.
- Head: small movements (initial yaw <= 5 degrees) with correct parallax/occlusion. A flat image rotated on canvas is **not acceptable**.
- Smile: mild, subtle cheek and eye-corner response, not only a mouth overlay.
- Listening: attentive gaze, occasional acknowledgment nod only when appropriate.
- Thinking: occasional look-away without dramatic 'loading' expressions.
- Speaking: keep head animation restrained; mouth motion is actually coupled to generated audio.
- When asked to stay still: suppress optional motion while retaining normal breathing/blink unless explicitly overridden.

## Language and demeanor
Voice target: English (US), friendly, clear and concise, genuine cadence and pauses. Do not assert that a particular TTS voice/model is available until integration is tested. Avoid theatrical gestures or constant nods.

## Avoid
- Nuance screenshot as identity source.
- Exaggerated doll-like eye size or glossy skin.
- Flat panning portrait mistaken for head rotation.
- Lip flapping based only on sound amplitude as final speech sync.
- Synthetic avatar called 'live' without functional microphone and speaker.
- Continuous expensive cloud video generation without a quantified benefit.
