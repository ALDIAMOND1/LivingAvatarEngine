# Nia — Original identity

Selected from the center panel of the original character collection. Nia has a distinct soft oval/rounded face, natural curly puff and loose tendrils, freckles across her cheeks and nose, a broad smile, gold studs, and an ivory cable-knit sweater. The photograph and eye-state asset package are now built from a matching new synthetic pose sheet; not from Nuance Labs imagery. This is a light photographic compositor, not a fully rigged 3D digital human.
